## 1. Geographic Scope & Thermodynamic Climate Profile

- **Target States (5 States + Regional Corridors):** Arizona, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, West Texas, Inland Southern California.
- **IECC / ASHRAE 90.1 Climate Zones:**
  - **Zone 2B (Hot - Dry):** Phoenix, Tucson, Yuma, Palm Springs, Imperial Valley. Extreme summer ambient temperatures exceeding 115°F to 120°F. Solar irradiance exceeding 1,000 W/m². High cooling degree days (CDD50 > 5,000). Attic ambient temperatures reaching 150°F to 165°F.
  - **Zone 3B (Warm - Dry):** Las Vegas, El Paso, St. George, low-altitude New Mexico. Extended triple-digit heat waves, low ambient humidity (5% to 15%), high nocturnal radiative cooling.
  - **Zone 4B / 5B (High Desert & Mountain Arid):** Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Flagstaff, Reno, Salt Lake City. High diurnal temperature swings (35°F to 50°F difference between afternoon high and nighttime low), intense high-altitude UV radiation, winter freezing combined with desert heat.
- **Core Building Physics & Structural Challenges:**
  - **Extreme Diurnal Thermal Shock & Stucco Cracking:** 40°F+ temperature swings within 12 hours causing aggressive expansion and contraction cycles on exterior cementitious stucco, parapets, and masonry joints, resulting in hairline fissures and structural spalling.
  - **Massive Solar Heat Gain & Attic Heat Storage:** Unshaded roofs absorbing intense solar radiation; heat radiates downward through unconditioned attic spaces into living areas, keeping HVAC systems running continuously through midnight.
  - **Expansive Caliche & Clay Soil Movement:** Deep desert soils containing dense calcium carbonate (caliche) and dry clay that violently swell when exposed to monsoon moisture, cracking slab-on-grade foundations, stem walls, and perimeter patios.
  - **Monsoon Downpours & Parapet Roof Flooding:** Intense summer convective thunderstorms dumping 2 inches of rain in 45 minutes; flat roofs with inadequate scupper sizing or clogged roof drains overflow, driving thousands of gallons into exterior framing cavities.
  - **Extreme UV Elastomeric Breakdown:** Relentless high-index UV radiation destroying traditional organic asphalt roofing shingles, caulks, and unrated plastic pipes in 3 to 7 years.
  - **HVAC Sensible Cooling Limits & Inverter Compressors:** Traditional single-stage AC units short-cycling or failing when outdoor temperatures cross 110°F; requirement for high-ambient inverter heat pumps and variable-speed scroll compressors capable of continuous operation at 122°F.

## 2. Dominant Regional Architectural Vernacular & Housing Archetypes

1. **Southwestern Adobe & Pueblo Revival (Historic to 1950):** Santa Fe, Albuquerque, Tucson. Sun-dried mud adobe bricks or stabilized rammed earth, massive 12-to-18-inch thick walls providing high thermal mass, exposed timber vigas and latillas, flat roofs with mud or lime parapets.
2. **Desert Modern Slab-on-Grade Stucco Tract (1970–Present):** Phoenix, Las Vegas, Tucson, Henderson. Monolithic post-tensioned concrete slab-on-grade, 2x4 or 2x6 wood framing, three-coat exterior Portland cement stucco with elastomeric paint, uninsulated concrete tile or asphalt shingle roof.
3. **Territorial & Spanish Mediterranean Tile (1980–Present):** Low-slope or 4:12 pitch roofs with S-tile or barrel clay/concrete tiles, vented attics with flexible ductwork baked in 150°F ambient heat, covered deep portales and covered ramadas.
4. **ICF & Rammed Earth Luxury Arid Custom (1995–Present):** Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Sedona, Park City. 10-inch thick Insulated Concrete Form walls or stabilized rammed earth delivering continuous R-30 insulation combined with high internal thermal capacitance.
5. **Manufactured Housing on Pier Blocks (Rural Desert):** High solar overheating, undersized ductwork running through unshaded metal belly wraps, frequent evaporative cooler (swamp cooler) corrosion.
